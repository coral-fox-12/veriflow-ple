# Verification phase

Your deliverables are written. Now verify them before you finish. Do not
trust your earlier work — re-derive it.

1. **Re-read every deliverable** you declared, top to bottom, from the
   container (not from your memory of writing it).
2. **Recompute every key number** with a fresh script that does not copy
   your earlier script's outputs. Compare the two results. If they differ
   by more than rounding, find out why and fix the deliverable.
3. **Unit check:** list every quantity with its unit. Convert inputs to SI
   base units in the script and confirm the output units match the
   deliverable. Flag any kN/N, mm/m, kVA/kW, or psi/Pa slip.
4. **Clause check:** for each code or standard claim, re-open the source
   document and confirm the clause number and the value.
5. **Completeness check:** confirm each deliverable is at the exact path
   and in the exact format the task asked for, and that no referenced
   figure, table, or appendix is missing.
6. **Fix, then re-verify.** Fix every discrepancy with your tools. If a
   deliverable cannot be fixed (missing input, unreadable drawing), state
   exactly what is missing and what you assumed instead.

When verification is done, reply without calling a tool: a short
verification report (what you checked, what you fixed, what remains
uncertain) followed by your final summary of the deliverables.
